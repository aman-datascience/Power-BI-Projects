# Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing Adidas US sales by month, state, region, product and retailer. Filters available for Year, Month and Sales Method.

## Dashboard Preview
![Dashboard](dashboard.png)

*Preview shows the 2020 view.*

## Key KPIs (2020)
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Sales | $182.08M |
| Total Profit | $63.38M |
| Units Sold | 3M |
| Avg Price per Unit | $50.63 |
| Profit Margin | 40.37% |

## Key Insights
- **West region** contributes the largest share of sales (42.23%), followed by Northeast (22.38%). Midwest is the lowest at 4.05%.
- **Men's Street Footwear** is the top product ($38M), followed by Women's Apparel ($35M) and Men's Athletic Footwear ($32M).
- **West Gear** is the top retailer ($90M), well ahead of Foot Locker ($43M) and Walmart ($33M). Kohl's contributes only about $1M.
- Sales **peak in April** (~$24M), **dip in June**, and recover in July-September.
- A profit margin above 40% shows strong overall profitability.

## Features
- Slicers: Year (2020/2021), Month, Sales Method (In-store / Online / Outlet)
- Charts: Area chart, Filled map, Donut chart, Bar charts
- KPI cards with icons
- "Clear all slicers" button
